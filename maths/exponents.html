Exponential functions are a way of descibing an exponential relationship between two numbers. Exponential relationships are when one number is too the power of another, and the variable is the power. The simplest exponential function is defined as $f(x)=2^x$. When an exponential function's x value increases its y value grows exponentialy. A visual graph is depicted to the right of what an exponential function might look like.

$$f(x)=2^x$$

Exponential functions have many compenents that can be changed and modified to change thier behavior. The general form of an exponential function is shown on the right. In this form, $b$ is the exponential base and controls the rate of growth of the function. $b$ also controls what the function is equal too when x=1 (as long as q is 0). We can predict what y will be at one by multiplying b by a. The rate of growth and y-intercept can be modified by changing $a$. $q$ is also a method of controlling the the y-intercept, except changing q will change the starting value of the exponential function.
