Concavity is the mathematical way of describing a function's shape and how it changes over time. Concavity is an important concept to understand when going further into calculus. Take the following graph for example.

You can tell a functions concavity by looking at its change in slope over time. In general, there is concave up and concave down. A concave down functions slope over time will be going DOWN. In our example, this would be everything before 0 (x > 0).

A concave UP function would be everything after 0 (x < 0) because after 0 the slope goes up over time.

(0, 0) is known as the switching point, when the function goes from DOWN to UP concave.

$$f(x)=x^3-3x$$
